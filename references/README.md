## What we take from the template

- Warm parchment background with paper grain and faint ink stains.
- Crimson brush-stroke circle behind a black-and-white cut-out photo of students.
- Classic serif display type for the headline, small serif nav (Home, Lesson, About, Location).
- Clean geometric sans for body copy.
- Four-point star ornaments, alone and in rows of six, as dividers.
- A thin horizontal rule running across the page behind the content.
- Pill-shaped maroon "JOIN NOW" button.
- Round social icons (Instagram, Facebook, X, WhatsApp) bottom right.

The template is a single hero screen. Every other section is ours to design, in
the same visual language. See `docs/architecture.md` for the full plan.
